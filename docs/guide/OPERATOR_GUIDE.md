# Operator handbook

For score operators, and for the admin who has to explain the job to them.
Everything here happens on a phone.

---

## Getting your account

An admin invites you by email. The invitation contains a one-time link:

1. Open the link. It expires, so use it the day it arrives.
2. Choose a password of at least 12 characters, twice.
3. You land on **Your matches**.

Nobody sets a password for you and nobody can see it. If the link has expired
or you never got it, ask the admin to send a fresh invitation.

Afterwards you sign in at `/staff/login` with your email and that password.
Ten wrong attempts in fifteen minutes locks sign-in for a while.

**Do this on the phone you will use on the day**, before the event, and check
that your matches appear.

---

## Your matches

The list shows only the matches you were given. Live matches come first, then
what is still to play, then anything postponed, then finished.

Each row gives the match code, the game and round, the players, the time and
table, and the status. **Not started** means nobody has begun it yet.
**Waiting for earlier results** means the players are still being decided by
an earlier match.

Search by match code or player name. Filter by **To play**, **Finished** or
**All**.

If the list is empty it says **No matches to play**. That is not a fault: it
means no assignment reaches you yet, or the draw has not been made. Ask the
admin.

You will never see anyone's payment details. The page says so at the top.

## Scoring a match

Open a match and press **Start match** when play begins. Recording the first
score starts it too, so there is no way to forget.

What you see next depends on the game. You never have to know the game's
"type" — the screen shows the right controls:

| Game                          | What you do                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Chess and similar             | Pick who won, or a draw, then confirm                              |
| eFootball, foosball           | Tap **Goal** under a player's name. Extra time and penalties too   |
| Table tennis, badminton       | Tap **Point** under a player. Sets are worked out for you          |
| Carrom, darts, pool           | Pick who won the board, set the points, press **Record board**     |
| 29 Cards, Ludo with four      | Type each player's points for the hand, press **Record hand**      |
| PUBG, Free Fire, racing       | Set each player's finishing position and tap **+** per elimination |
| Scrabble, cube times, quizzes | Type each player's final number and press **Save** on their row    |

Every screen also has a way to **type the score in directly** if you fell
behind, and an **Undo** button on each line of the match log.

### Finishing

Press **Finalize result**, check the preview line, then **Yes, confirm final
result**. After that only an admin can reopen it, so read the score first.

If your level is **Score only**, the screen tells you your assignment does not
include confirming, and an admin does that step.

### Somebody did not turn up

Open **A competitor did not show up**, tick whoever is absent, add a short
note and press **Record walkover**. The one player present wins. If nobody
came, nobody advances.

## When things go wrong

**You lose signal.** Nothing is lost. The bar at the top says _Offline.
Actions are kept on this device and sent when the connection returns_, and
they send themselves the moment you are back. They are stored in the phone's
browser, so they survive a reload, a crash or a flat battery.

**Never re-enter the same score on another phone.** Wait for the first one to
catch up.

**Someone else scored the same match.** You will see _Someone else updated
this match. Check the latest score and confirm again._ The screen shows what
is actually recorded. Nothing you entered was silently overwritten; look at
the score, then act.

**The bar says an action needs your attention.** One item could not be saved,
and the queue has stopped so nothing goes in the wrong order. Read its
message, then either **Send now** or **Dismiss** it.

**You opened the match in two tabs.** Only one can send. The other offers
**Score here instead**.

**You get signed out.** Your queued actions stay on the phone. Sign in again
in another tab and send them.

**The match is postponed.** Scoring stops until an admin resumes it.

### Reporting a problem

Open **Report a problem** on the match, choose the kind (no-show, score
dispute, equipment, conduct, timing, or something else) and describe it in a
sentence. Admins see it straight away and it does not change the score. For
something not about one match, there is a reporter at the bottom of your match
list.

When an admin resolves it, their note appears on your screen.

## What your access means

An admin gives you either a whole game, a round, a single match, or every
match one player is in. Whatever you were given, you get one of three levels:

| Level                         | You can                                   |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Score and confirm results** | Enter scores and confirm the final result |
| **Score only**                | Enter scores; an admin confirms           |
| **Watch only**                | Watch, and report a problem               |

Everyone can report a problem.

If your access is taken away mid-match, the screen tells you: _You are not
assigned to this match any more._ If your account is deactivated you are
signed out and cannot sign back in until an admin reactivates you, and they
then have to give you your games again.
