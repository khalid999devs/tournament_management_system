# Participant UI Direction

The participant experience uses the existing NDCAK identity rather than copying the reference screens literally.

## Structure adopted from the references

- Persistent primary navigation with clear active-route treatment.
- A concise announcement rail for high-priority event information.
- Split homepage hero: campaign message on the left, scannable game cards on the right.
- Strong registration steps with grouped personal details, event selection, review, and payment states.
- Schedule pages with day filters, a primary timeline area, and supporting event details.
- Rulebook pages with section navigation and a readable document surface.
- A complete footer with official contact details and quick links.

## NDCAK visual treatment

- Deep navy surfaces, ivory text, institutional gold actions, and green status cues.
- Barlow Condensed for expressive headings; Inter for body copy and controls.
- Square and lightly rounded geometry, restrained borders, and grid-based rhythm.
- High contrast without neon colors, decorative emoji, or invented event data.
- Mobile layouts preserve the same information order and use horizontally scrollable navigation where needed.

## Content rules

- Dates, fees, capacities, prizes, payment accounts, and schedules come from approved configuration only.
- Pending information is labeled honestly rather than represented with sample values.
- Participant submission language always distinguishes pending review from confirmation.
